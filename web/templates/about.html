Methodology

How an edition gets made.

Six stages, ~5 minutes of compute per quarter. Every claim about a competitor traces back to a verbatim earnings-call quote. Every claim about Wego traces back to a sanctioned profile fact.

What makes the implications trustworthy

Negative space

The synthesis prompt enumerates what Wego does not have — no direct booking funnel, no owned LLM, no voice interface, no car-rental vertical. This kills 80% of "modern travel app" hallucinations the model would otherwise invent.

Refusal quota

If the firm's commentary touches a theme outside Wego's surface area, the brief must include an explicit "no direct read for Wego on [topic]". Absence is named, not filled.

Critique pass

A second LLM call audits the brief and flags hallucinations by severity. High-severity claims are auto-rewritten by the fixup pass before the brief is published.

Tested ceiling

Run-loop calibration: 17 hallucinations in baseline → 0 in tier 3, across 8 runs over 4 companies. The pipeline is at the empirical floor of what this stack can produce.

What's not automated yet

  • TCOM transcripts — Trip.com is a Chinese ADR; US transcript aggregators don't cover it consistently. Fetched manually when needed.
  • Semantic deduplication — if two firms say nearly the same thing, both currently appear. A diffing pass could collapse those.
  • Time-series view — once two editions exist, a side-by-side delta view will show what changed quarter-over-quarter.
