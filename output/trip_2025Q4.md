## Tripadvisor (TRIP) · 2025Q4

*Tripadvisor is pushing an AI‑native travel assistant while emphasizing that the planning‑to‑booking confidence gap remains wide and its marketing spend stays disciplined.*

### What the firm said
Tripadvisor highlighted a strategic pivot toward AI, noting that “almost half of travelers use AI to plan trips, but less than 10% are actually booking with AI” and positioning the “confidence gap” as a core growth lever. The company unveiled an “AI‑native MVP” that will leverage its data to deliver “more relevant, personalized recommendations” and stressed that it is “AI first, AI native” in partnership with OpenAI, Amazon, Snap and Microsoft. Conversions from the AI‑native experience are “a bit stronger because it’s relatively higher intent, lower down the funnel.” On the monetisation side, the CFO said marketing remains “very large” but awareness is “still relatively low,” and that “the marketing spend was kinda in line with our historical disciplined approach.” SEO is described as a minor driver for the experiences business, “expected … to be below 10%,” and the CEO argued that this “insulates from AI disintermediation” because the model is less dependent on “structurally challenged SEO traffic.”  

### What this means for Wego
Tripadvisor’s AI‑native push has no direct read for Wego — Wego remains a referrals‑only metasearch platform without an AI assistant or LLM partnership. The firm’s claim that lower‑SEO reliance “insulates” from AI disintermediation underscores the continued strategic value of Wego’s organic‑search moat in MENA, where SEO is a primary acquisition channel. Tripadvisor’s disciplined marketing spend, described as “in line with historical approach,” offers no direct read for Wego on paid‑media intensity; Wego’s growth model stays anchored in brand‑driven, cost‑efficient organic traffic.
